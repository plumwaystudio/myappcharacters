# Writing skits that animate well

These rules keep the cast consistent and stick to what this engine does best: front-facing PS2 cutscene acting, driven by the voice lines.

## Format
- 15–20 seconds, 6–8 shots, one line per shot.
- Each line is 12 words or fewer. Numbers are written as words for the voices.
- No more than 2 lines per character. LOWKEY always gets the last deadpan line (the "button").
- Structure: pov hook → setup → it gets worse → twist → LOWKEY's button → app reveal.

## Pacing and the phone
- **Don't cut too fast.** Hold each shot at least 2 seconds; the only shorter ones are the reveal stingers (phone insert and freeze reaction, about 1 second each). Put a short silent beat before the reveal line so it lands.
- **The app always looks clean.** The phone screen is drawn sharp at full resolution *after* the PS2 filter, never pixelated or grainy. Real UI, readable numbers, "Example numbers" on screen.

## Stage marks (characters never move from these)
Wide shot, left to right: **LOWKEY · Nia · Bri · Klarna Kid**, standing in a shallow arc and facing the camera, turned slightly toward the center. Characters turn their head or upper body, but they never walk, sit or swap places.

## Shot codes (use only these)
| Code | What it is |
|---|---|
| `WIDE` | All four on their marks (use once, to set up the scene) |
| `MID-<name>` | Waist-up, one character, three-quarter front |
| `CU-<name>` | Face close-up, front or three-quarter (turned 35° at most) |
| `SIDE-LOWKEY>name` | Medium side angle of LOWKEY turning toward someone (no backs of heads) |
| `REACT-<name>` | Close-up freeze reaction: brows up, eyes wide, prop shakes |
| `INSERT-PHONE` | Full-screen phone showing the real app UI with example numbers |
| `END` | App screen, "LINK IN MY STORY", LOWKEY voiceover |

## Actions that look good
Talking, nodding, head tilt, side-eye, raised brow, smirk or smile, blink, freeze, sip from a held cup, hair flip (head tilt), raising the phone or turning it to show the screen, scrolling at chest height, pointing, spinning keys, LOWKEY sliding his glasses down.

## Avoid (write around these)
Characters touching each other (hugs, daps, handing things over), sitting or standing up, walking or running, backs or full profiles of faces, eating, crowds or extra people, outfit changes in an episode, more than one new prop per episode.

## Signature props (always on them)
- **Bri:** giant pink tumbler (right hand), yoga mat on her back
- **Nia:** phone (right hand), yellow charm bag
- **LOWKEY:** phone, film camera around his neck, round glasses
- **Klarna Kid:** car keys (right hand), gold chain

## Sets
Built: **living room** (wood-panel wall, posters, shelf, couch). A new set costs extra build time, so use at most one per episode and keep it simple (a wall, floor and 2–3 props).

## Storyboard line format
`time | shot code | who speaks | line | action/expression | sound`

Example:
`0:00–0:03 | MID-bri | Bri | I'm budgeting for the trip. | sips tumbler, unbothered | ice rattle`
